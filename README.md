## About me

I am a meteorologist and HPC system architect at Taiwan's Central Weather Administration, working at the intersection of operational meteorology, numerical weather prediction, and large-scale scientific computing.

My background is in atmospheric science and cloud microphysics, with experience in MM5 and WRF. I previously led global data-assimilation work for more than a decade, contributing to ensemble typhoon-track prediction and hybrid methods including 3DEnVar and 4DEnVar.

Today, I focus on reliable HPC and NWP systems while exploring machine learning, LLMs, and retrieval-augmented generation for weather forecasting, disaster response, and scientific operations.

> **Weather-trained. Infrastructure-minded. AI-forward.**

- **[Slurm Toolbox](https://github.com/DengShunChen/slrum-toolbox)** — Operations and analytics tools for cluster health checks, usage reports, and job workflows.
