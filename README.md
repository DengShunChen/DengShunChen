## About me

I am a meteorologist and HPC system architect at Taiwan's Central Weather Administration, working at the intersection of operational meteorology, numerical weather prediction, and large-scale scientific computing.

My background is in atmospheric science and cloud microphysics, with experience using MM5 and WRF. I previously led global data-assimilation work for more than a decade, contributing to ensemble typhoon-track prediction and hybrid methods including 3DEnVar and 4DEnVar.

Today, I focus on reliable, scalable HPC and NWP systems while exploring how machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, and retrieval-augmented generation can strengthen weather forecasting, disaster response, and scientific operations.

> Weather-trained. Infrastructure-minded. AI-forward.

## Current focus

- **HPC operations** — Slurm, GPU systems, storage performance, monitoring, and production reliability
- **Numerical weather prediction** — data assimilation, ensemble prediction, WRF, MPAS, and UFS
- **AI for weather** — domain corpora, RAG, LLMs, and GPU-accelerated training
- **Scientific software** — Fortran, C/C++, Python, CUDA, MPI, OpenMP, OpenACC, and Spack
- **Knowledge sharing** — practical HPC education for scientific-computing teams
